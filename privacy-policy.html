Privacy Policy

Vishal Sir's Maths Classes · Etawah, Uttar Pradesh, India

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Vishal Sir's Maths Classes (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website, contact us, or submit an enquiry about our maths coaching classes for Classes 8–10.

1. Information we may collect

When you contact us or enquire about classes, you may provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, class/grade, and the details of your enquiry. Please provide only information needed for your enquiry.

Our website also uses links or embedded services provided by third parties. Those services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser/device information, and activity according to their own policies.

2. How we use information

We use information you choose to provide to:

  • respond to questions and coaching enquiries;
  • contact you about class availability, admission, and relevant class information;
  • operate, protect, and improve our website and communications; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Enquiry forms, WhatsApp, and other third-party services

The enquiry button on our website opens a Google Forms page. If you submit that form, the information is handled through Google’s services and may be subject to Google's privacy policy. Review Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Our website also links to WhatsApp so you can message us. If you choose to use WhatsApp, your use of that service is subject to WhatsApp's terms and privacy policy at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

The website may display a Google Maps map or link to Google Maps. Google may process information when you load or use its map service; see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may receive or share information through the third-party services you choose to use (such as Google Forms or WhatsApp), with service providers needed to operate our communications, or where disclosure is required by law. We do not intentionally disclose enquiry details for unrelated advertising.

5. Data retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, manage class-related communications, maintain appropriate records, or meet legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information you have provided to us, subject to applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements. Contact us using the phone number below and describe the information or enquiry you want us to locate. Information already submitted directly to Google Forms or WhatsApp may also need to be managed through those services.

7. Children's privacy

Our coaching classes are for school students, including minors. Parents or guardians should submit enquiries on behalf of younger students where appropriate and should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information through the website or messaging services.

8. Links to other websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review the privacy policies of any third-party service you choose to use.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page, with the effective date updated above.
